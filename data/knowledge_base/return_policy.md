# Return & Refund Policy

## Return Eligibility

Items are eligible for return if:
- The return is requested within **30 days** of delivery.
- The item is in its **original condition** — unused, unwashed, with all original tags and packaging.
- The item is not in our non-returnable category (see below).

**Non-returnable items:**
- Perishable goods (food, flowers, plants)
- Digital downloads and software licenses
- Intimate apparel and swimwear (for hygiene reasons)
- Hazardous materials
- Gift cards

---

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Log into your account and go to **Order History**.
2. Select the order containing the item you want to return.
3. Click **"Request Return"** and select the reason for return.
4. Print the prepaid return shipping label (emailed to you within 1 hour).
5. Pack the item securely and drop it off at any authorized carrier location.
6. Your refund will be processed within **5-7 business days** of us receiving the item.

---

## Refund Methods

| Original Payment Method | Refund Method | Processing Time |
|------------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Credit/Debit Card | Back to original card | 5–7 business days |
| PayPal | Back to PayPal balance | 3–5 business days |
| Store Credit | Store credit added | Immediate |
| Bank Transfer | Bank transfer | 7–10 business days |

---

## Damaged or Defective Items

If you received a damaged or defective item:
- Contact support **within 48 hours** of delivery.
- Provide your order ID and clear photos of the damage.
- We will offer one of the following: full refund, replacement item, or store credit — your choice.
- You do **not** need to return damaged items in most cases.

---

## Wrong Item Received

If you received the wrong item:
- Contact support with your order ID and a photo of the item received.
- We will ship the correct item at no extra charge.
- A prepaid return label will be provided for the incorrect item.
- You have 14 days to return the wrong item.

---

## Late or Missing Refunds

If you have not received your refund after the stated processing time:
1. Check your bank account or PayPal balance again.
2. Contact your bank — processing times can vary.
3. If still unresolved, contact our support team with your order ID and refund request ID.

---

## Exchange Policy

We do not currently offer direct exchanges. To get a different size, color, or product:
1. Return the original item following the process above.
2. Place a new order for the desired item.

This ensures the fastest processing time for both transactions.

---

## Return Shipping Costs

- **Defective/damaged/wrong items**: Free return shipping (we provide the label).
- **Change of mind returns**: Customer pays return shipping ($5.99 flat rate via our carrier, deducted from refund).
- **International returns**: Customer pays return shipping. Customs fees are non-refundable.
