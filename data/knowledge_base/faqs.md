# Frequently Asked Questions

## Order Tracking

**Q: How can I track my order?**
A: You can track your order by visiting our website and clicking "Track Order" in the top menu. Enter your order ID and email address to see real-time status updates. You will also receive automated email updates at every stage of your shipment.

**Q: My order says "processing" for more than 2 days. Is this normal?**
A: Orders typically process within 24 hours. If your order has been in "processing" status for more than 2 business days, it may indicate a payment verification delay or a stock issue. Please contact support with your order ID and we will investigate immediately.

**Q: I received a tracking number but it shows no updates.**
A: Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to appear after the label is created. If there are still no updates after 48 hours, the package may be delayed at the carrier facility. Contact support with your tracking number and we will escalate with the carrier.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?**
A: Address changes are only possible if the order has not yet been shipped. If your order is still in "processing" status, contact support immediately with your order ID and the new address. Once shipped, address changes are not possible.

## Account & Login

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your registered email address. You will receive a reset link within 5 minutes. Check your spam folder if you do not see it. The link expires after 1 hour.

**Q: How do I update my email address?**
A: Log into your account, go to Account Settings > Personal Information, and click "Edit" next to your email. You will need to verify the new email address before the change takes effect.

**Q: My account has been locked. What should I do?**
A: Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts for security purposes. Wait 30 minutes for automatic unlock, or contact support to unlock it immediately. We may ask you to verify your identity.

## Payments & Billing

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All transactions are encrypted and secure.

**Q: Why was my payment declined?**
A: Common reasons include: insufficient funds, incorrect card details, card expiry, or bank security blocks on online transactions. Try a different payment method or contact your bank. If the issue persists, contact our support team.

**Q: I was charged twice for one order.**
A: Double charges are usually pre-authorization holds that resolve within 3-5 business days. If both charges appear as completed transactions, contact support immediately with your order ID and bank statement screenshot. We will resolve this within 24 hours.

## Products

**Q: How do I know if a product is in stock?**
A: Product pages show real-time stock status. "In Stock" means immediate availability. "Low Stock" means fewer than 5 units remain. "Out of Stock" items can be added to your wishlist for restock notifications.

**Q: The product I received is different from what was shown online.**
A: We apologize for this inconvenience. Please take photos of the received product and contact support with your order ID. We will arrange a free return and send the correct item or issue a full refund.

**Q: Can I request a product that is not listed on the website?**
A: We do not currently offer custom orders. However, you can submit product requests through our feedback form and our team reviews all requests monthly.
