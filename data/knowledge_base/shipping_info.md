---

## Free Shipping Policy

- Free standard shipping on all domestic orders over **$50**.
- Free shipping promotions during sale events (Black Friday, seasonal sales) are automatically applied at checkout.
- Free shipping does not apply to oversized items (marked on product page).

---

## Order Processing Time

- In-stock items: **24 hours** (Monday–Friday).
- Pre-order items: Ships on the stated release date.
- Custom/personalized items: 3–5 business days before shipping.
- During peak seasons (holidays), processing may take up to **48 hours**.

## Tracking Your Shipment

1. You will receive a tracking email as soon as your order ships.
2. Click the tracking link in the email or visit our website's "Track Order" page.
3. Enter your order ID or tracking number.
4. Real-time updates are available from pickup to delivery.

**Tracking not updating?** Carrier systems can take up to 24 hours to reflect the first scan. If there are no updates after 48 hours, contact support.

---

## International Shipping

**Countries we ship to:** Over 50 countries worldwide. Check at checkout if your country is supported.

**Customs & Duties:**
- International orders may be subject to customs fees, import duties, and taxes.
- These charges are determined by your country's customs authority and are the responsibility of the customer.
- We declare the actual value of goods on all customs forms — we cannot mark items as "gift" to reduce duties.

**Restricted Items:**
- Some products cannot be shipped internationally due to regulations (batteries, certain chemicals).
- Restricted items will show "Not available for international shipping" on the product page.

---

## Delivery Issues

**Package marked delivered but not received:**
1. Check with neighbors and building management.
2. Check around all entry points (back door, garage).
3. Wait 24 hours — carriers sometimes mark as delivered early.
4. If still missing, contact support within **5 days** of the marked delivery date. We will file a carrier claim.

**Package damaged on arrival:**
- Take photos of the damaged packaging and contents before opening fully.
- Contact support within **48 hours** with photos and order ID.
- We will arrange replacement or refund — no return of damaged items required.

**Package lost in transit:**
- If tracking shows no updates for more than 7 days, contact support.
- We will initiate a carrier trace. Resolution typically takes 5–10 business days.
- If confirmed lost, we will reship or refund in full.

---

## Address Issues

**Incorrect address entered at checkout:**
- Contact support **immediately** with the correct address.
- If the order has not shipped, we can update the address at no charge.
- If already shipped, we cannot redirect most packages in transit.

**Failed delivery attempts:**
- Carriers typically make 3 delivery attempts.
- After 3 failed attempts, the package is held at a local facility for **7 days**.
- If unclaimed, it is returned to us. We will contact you for reshipment (additional shipping fee may apply).
